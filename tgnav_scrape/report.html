How this was built, and what it can’t tell you

What counts as “a channel”

The repo publishes a built site, not a database, so the channel list has to be read back out of the HTML. Two eras need two readers: before 2024-09-30 the whole directory is the card grid on index.html (data-url per card, quoted then unquoted after minification); from 2024-09-30 on it is one directory per channel under detail/.

Handles are matched case-insensitively, since Telegram usernames are. Promoted airport/VPN adverts — cards pointing at a domain rather than a t.me handle — are excluded.

Where the seams are

The 2024-09-30 rebuild is a measurement change as well as a content change: the old homepage was the entire published directory, the new site publishes detail pages. Its +118 is a real jump in what the site showed, but not 118 separate editorial decisions.

One commit, 清除页面（路由错误） on 2026-07-15, published a broken build with no pages. It is skipped so it doesn’t register as a mass deletion; the fix landed a minute later.

Categories are current-only

Category pages such as channel/机场测试/ exist only in the July 2026 site, so channels can be placed in a category as it stands today, not historically. All 37 channels in 机场测试 arrived with that rebuild — the category has no history before it.

112 channels that are live today are not linked from any listing page; they show as uncategorised here because the site itself doesn’t file them anywhere.

Churn is slower than the totals suggest

Of the 203 channels present at the first commit, 101 are still listed. Of the 835 added since, 725 survive. Removals are rarely quick corrections: the median removed channel was listed for 645 days, and only 27 lasted under 90.

32 channels were removed and later re-listed; the table shows their first and last appearance, and their ×2 style marker counts how many separate stints they had.
